Recipe for
Creating Your
Creature

LET'S MAKE ONE ↓
✿

Ingredients

1 sheet of paper or a tablet
Drawing tools

  1. 01

    Draw one large shape of your choice to be its body.

  2. 02

    Make its eyes your favorite colors. They can be different colors.

  3. 03

    Determine the number of legs.

    ☼If you ate breakfast today4 legs
    ◒If you skipped breakfast3 legs
    ?If you're not sure6 legs
  4. 04

    Add fur.

    ✿Wearing a jacket, sweater, or coat?Give it fur
    ▧OtherwiseGive it scales
  5. 05

    Draw the ears of your favorite animal.

    ♧
  6. 06

    If you wear glasses, give it a tail.

  7. 07

    Color the creature's body with colors from anything that you are seeing right now.

  8. 08

    Add anything else of your choice and complete the drawing.

    ✧
